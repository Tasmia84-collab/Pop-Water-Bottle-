Why We Are Called POP — And What "Pop Mean" Stands For

Our brand is strictly POP (P-O-P) — never "Pop Up" — anchored by our signature slogan and full-form philosophy: "Pop Mean" (Pure Optimized Personalized Water).

For years, businesses, wedding families, hotels, and event organizers wanted their own name or logo printed on pure drinking water bottles. Traditional bottling plants demanded 10,000+ bottle commitments. POP ("Pop Mean") broke that barrier by engineering an agile digital-wrap & sterile filling line that delivers bespoke 330ml, 500ml, 1 Liter, and 1.5 Liter bottles starting from just 50 or 100 bottles.
